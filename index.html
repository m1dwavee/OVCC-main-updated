Who We Are

About Orvion Convoy Control

Orvion Convoy Control is a rapidly growing team providing professional convoy control services within the TruckersMP community.

Our team was established to ensure that events run in an organized, safe, and smooth manner; we aim to deliver high-standard services in traffic management, convoy control, and event support.

With our strong team structure, active staff, and commitment to professionalism, we strive to contribute to events of all sizes in the best possible way.

Our vision as Orvion Convoy Control is to become a respected team representing reliability, discipline, and quality, while continuously improving ourselves.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I request convoy control for an upcoming event?
Join our Discord server and open a ticket under Event Supervision. Provide your event date, route map, and expected player count.
2. What games and servers does Orvion CC support?
We support Euro Truck Simulator 2 (ETS2) and American Truck Simulator (ATS) across all official SCS Multiplayer and TruckersMP servers.
3. How do I apply for the CC Recruitment team?
We welcome active CC! Submit your application in our discord!
